A corporate law firm built as one team.

Martand Legal Advisors was started in early 2013 by young lawyers who wanted every corporate legal service to be available in one place.

OUR STORY

Started with a simple idea.

A growing business should not need a different adviser for every question. Our work today covers corporate law and compliance, commercial agreements, intellectual property, FEMA, corporate advisory, mergers and acquisitions and employee stock options.

Behind that is a team of lawyers, chartered accountants, company secretaries, intellectual property experts and registered valuers who keep up with changes in the law and bring them to each matter.

From our foundation in Hyderabad, we have expanded to Indore, Pune, and Bengaluru—working as one unified national bench for founders, institutions, and global investors.
